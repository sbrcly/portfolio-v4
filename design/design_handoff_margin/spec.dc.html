Two sources of truth, four consumers, no new elements. Everything below is implemented live on home-1440 and prava-1440 as the reference; the storyboards show the timings frame by frame.

Revision 1 · two corrections, one base change
Base
Mockups rebuilt against main (app/, components/, app/tokens.css): labels, spacing, rim values and the chapter observer are the live ones. The hero has no top row of any kind. Email sits below the statement and in the footer.
1 · Margin from the top
The "no margin on the first screen" rule is dropped. At scroll zero the margin shows I and Home, dim, exactly like II to IV. The hero is a chapter: 280 + 64 + measure, the measure holding rule, name, statement, email, centred vertically; min-height 100svh.
2 · State at load
currentChapter is I at first paint (current-chapter.ts starts at chapters[0]); the nav lights I Home and the margin shows I with no animation. The band observer resolves I because the hero, at 100svh, always intersects the 40 to 60% band at scroll zero. Prava: the title chapter reads "Prava" with the numeral slot empty (see Position, 3c).

State model

currentChapter
'i' | 'ii' | 'iii' | 'iv' · initial 'i' · Prava: 'prava-title' | '01'…'05'

Written by one IntersectionObserver over the [data-chapter] elements, rootMargin -40% 0px -40% 0px, threshold 0 (components/chapters/current-chapter.ts, unchanged). Initial value is the first chapter. When two intersect the band at once, the later one in document order wins. Equivalent statement for the engineer: the current chapter is the last section whose top is above the viewport's 60% line.

plate rim · resting ↔ lit box-shadow

The margin never computes anything. It renders two values other things already own, so it cannot disagree with the nav or with the light. Hover changes nothing in the margin; pointer-events are off.

Handover · the way the light changes

Out · 0 to 400
Opacity 1 → 0, numeral #4A5A50 → #2E3B34, label #A89F90 → #4A5A50. ease. The content cools before it is gone.
Nothing · 400 to 600
Empty margin. The incoming content is mounted at opacity 0, #2E3B34, during this window.
In · 600 to 1200
Opacity 0 → 1, colour cold → resting. cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1). No transform of any kind.
Load
The margin renders I and Home in the server markup; nothing fades in. Under the entrance veil it is simply there when the veil lifts, like the rest of the hero. The first thing that ever animates in the margin is the I to II handover.
Interrupted
A new event during a handover cancels the pending timers; whatever is on screen runs its 400 out, then the newest target comes in. Fast scrolling through II shows a brief II, never a half-II-half-III.
Second line
Its own fader on the litPlate source, identical timings, so it fades with the plate rim it describes. The numeral and label above it do not react to entry changes.
Reduced motion
Outgoing removed at 0, nothing until 200, incoming at full at 200. Plate rims swap at the same instants. No transitions anywhere in the margin.

Chapter boundaries · exactly when

Scrolling down
Chapter N+1 becomes current the frame its section's top edge crosses the 60% line. Nav cell and margin fader fire from that one callback.
Scrolling up
Chapter N becomes current again when N+1's top edge crosses back below the 60% line. Same line both ways; no hysteresis on chapters (the band's 20% height is the hysteresis).
Scroll zero
Hero is 100svh, so it alone fills the band: currentChapter = I, nav I Home lit, margin I Home. Scrolling back up into I is an ordinary handover (400 / 200 / 600) back to I.
Prava top
prava-title (title block + screens plate, one data-chapter on main) is current at scroll zero. Margin: numeral slot empty, label "Prava". At 01 the label changes and the numeral fades in above it; the label's y does not move.
Deep link
Landing on #iii: server markup shows I; the observer's first callback moves it to III. Treat that as a load, not a handover: swap without animation if it happens before the entrance veil lifts (or within the first 300 ms with no veil), so a deep link never shows I cooling.
Entry in III
Second line appears when the first plate takes the light, which at 1440 × 900 is about 180 px after III's opener enters the band (plate 01's top is on screen by then). It is empty while the opener alone is in view and empty again after plate 05 has scrolled off, so the margin reads "III Work" alone at both ends of the chapter.

Markup and accessibility

The margin
One div aria-hidden="true", pointer-events none, outside the sections, rendered from state. Nothing focusable, nothing announced. It duplicates what the nav and the headings already say.
The h2
Stays in the opener's left cell in document order, visually hidden at ≥ 960 (1 px clip, not display none), so reading order and aria-labelledby are exactly round 2's. Below 960 it is the visible label beside the in-flow numeral, as built.
Hero
section#i: grid 280 + 64 + 1fr, align-items center, min-height 100svh, padding 120 0. Measure: lit rule (data-light="i", data-lit at first paint), h1, statement (max 720), then the email as a mono 12 link with a rule-colour underline, 40 below. No h2, no top row, no location, no availability. Below 720: inline 72 numeral and Home label above the rule, as the other stacked openers.
In-flow numeral
Removed at ≥ 960, hero included. The pinned numeral is the only numeral, so there is never a moment with two on screen.
Data
section[data-chapter] for the band observer; article[data-plate][data-entry] for the light, data-entry being the second-line text verbatim ("02 Live odds console"). Prava sections add data-label.
Contrast
Numeral #4A5A50 on #121A16 is 2.3:1, allowed as in round 2 because it is decorative and aria-hidden. Label and second line #A89F90, 6.3:1.
Not added
No progress line, no traveling tick, no scroll indicator, no background, no border, no hover state, no glow. The nav is unchanged.
