What it does

Present means present on any of eight surfaces, once.

Placeholder. Eight surfaces in the app can show that a person was there: the journal, prayer, scripture, the lectionary, the memory verse, a circle, a commitment, teaching. Each writes its own records, in UTC. The dashboard folds all eight into one row per person per local day, and every widget on it reads that fold. None reads a leg directly.

Placeholder. The fold is by local day, so the moment is bucketed in the person's timezone before the union, not after. A late-night prayer in one city and an early journal in another are the same day for the person they belong to, which is the only day that matters here.

Cost
AI usage events per surface, checked against the cache metrics the provider reports
Markers
A definition change is drawn on the chart the day it lands
Data
Demo data in every capture; the real figures stay off the internet

What was hard

Changing what a number means without lying about the past.

Placeholder. When the definition of present widened from two surfaces to eight, every chart would have shown a jump that was not growth. The fix was a marker: the day the definition changed is drawn on the chart, and the series on either side is labelled with the definition it was counted under. Nothing is backfilled.

Placeholder. The second hard part was cost. A usage-event table in Postgres records every AI call with its tokens, its surface, and what the provider reported about caching, so the three waves of cost work on the Prava page can be read as a chart rather than claimed.
