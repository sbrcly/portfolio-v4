Entrance · "Ember" · 1400 ms · once per session · skippable

An overlay on top of content that is already in the DOM. Keyed on sessionStorage. Click, tap, Esc, or any key skips to the handoff. The light ends on the hero's lit rule, not the name. A working reference implementation runs on home-1440 (tweaks: replay, reduced motion).

0 to 400 ms
Veil is the ground color, nothing else. A 2px rule, 120px wide, draws from its center outward: scaleX 0 to 1, hot brass #F3C77E, glow at peak (0 0 10px .9, 0 0 32px .5). cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1).
|SB|
300 to 700 ms
The bracket mark fades in above the rule, bone, no glow, opacity 0 to 1, linear. The rule is the only lit thing on screen.
|SB|
700 to 1000 ms
The rule cools: #F3C77E to #E0AE62, glow to zero. ease-in-out. An ember settling. Mark holds at full.
1000 to 1400 ms
Veil opacity 1 to 0, cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1). At 1250 the hero's rule (flat brass until now) warms to #F3C77E and takes its glow over 150 ms. The ember moves from the mark to the page.

1400 ms
Overlay removed from the DOM. Skip jumps to 1000 and runs the last 400 ms. Nothing on the page moved; the only state change is the hero rule's color.
|SB|
Reduced motion
Mark and rule appear together at full, flat brass, no draw, no color change. Hold 900 ms, veil fades 300 ms (opacity only). Hero rule is lit from first paint. Total 1200 ms, same skip rules.

Dim chapter numerals · decorative, never the heading

Markup: the mono label is the h2 and the section's aria-labelledby target; the numeral is a span aria-hidden="true". The nav carries the numeral and the chapter name as one link text, so a screen reader hears "II About" once, in the nav, and "About" as the heading. The numeral's 2.3:1 contrast is allowed because it conveys nothing the label does not.

Motion · unchanged from round 1 plus the plate handover

One lit thing
Home: the rule. About: nothing glows (the chapter is text). Work: exactly one plate rim. Contact: the email. The handover between any two is cool 400 ms, dark 200 ms, warm 600 ms, cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), never overlapping.
Plate to plate
Resting rim: 0 0 0 1px rgba(224,174,98,.35), 0 0 72px rgba(224,174,98,.10). Lit rim: 0 0 0 1px rgba(224,174,98,.6), 0 0 96px rgba(224,174,98,.18). The lit plate is the one whose center is nearest the viewport center, measured by an IntersectionObserver with thresholds every 10% plus a scroll-end check. Hysteresis: a new plate must be at least 10% of viewport height nearer the center than the current one before the light moves. Transition is on the shadow alpha (animate a CSS variable, not box-shadow keyframes), 600 ms up, 400 ms down, with the 200 ms dark gap enforced by delaying the incoming plate.
390, two plates visible
At 390 the plates are short (about 208px for 16:9) and two can share the viewport. Same rule, nearest center wins, with the hysteresis preventing a flip while a thumb is still on the glass. While the user is actively scrolling (scroll events within the last 120 ms) no handover starts; it resolves on scroll end. If neither plate is within 35% of center (reading a caption between two plates), the current one stays lit rather than going dark.
Video and light
A playing video forces its plate lit regardless of position. On end it releases and the nearest-center rule resumes.
Reveals
Opener numeral opacity 0 to 1 over 900 ms, no movement. Measure and plate: opacity and 12px rise, 600 ms, staggered 120 / 200 ms. Fire once at 20% visibility, unobserve, never replay. Hidden pre-state applied by JS to below-fold elements only.
Reduced motion
No translate; reveals 200 ms opacity; rim handover is an instant swap after the 200 ms gap; no video autoplay; entrance per the reduced variant above.
