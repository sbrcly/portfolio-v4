Round 2Hero optionsREADME

Two ways to resolve the dark above the name. Each at 1440 by 900 and 1920 by 1080.

Neither is centered and neither adds an image. Option A is used on the home pages in this round.

A

Anchored to the middle, chapter row on top

The lit rule sits at the optical center of the viewport. The upper band carries the chapter header row (I Home, a rule, email, location, availability) in mono, so the dark above the name is bounded by a line the reader has already read. The name and statement hang from the rule.

B

Low name, plumb line

The name stays in the lower third. The upper band carries the ruled mono line, and from its left end a 1px vertical hairline drops to the lit rule: a plumb line that gives the eye a path through the dark and makes the empty space read as measured rather than unfilled. A small mono column beside the line lists the chapters as a table of contents.
