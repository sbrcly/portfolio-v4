Rules

  • Autoplay: muted, starts when 60% of the plate is in view, pauses when it leaves, never restarts from zero on re-entry. Reduced motion or Save-Data: no autoplay, resting state only.
  • Phone (390): the plate bleeds to the viewport edge; the play ring is 52px; meta is 10px; the poster frame is the same image. Tap toggles play; controls appear on tap, hide after 2 s idle.
  • Accessibility: the plate is a button labeled "Play the odds console recording, 33 seconds, silent"; while playing the label changes to "Pause". Captions are not needed (no audio), stated in the label.
